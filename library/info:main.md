**Note that this is only a demo of the app we want to develop. None of the features is ready to use yet.**

Making sure you make the best consumer decisions, staying on top of sustainability news and comparing
available product alternatives requires a lot of time for research and can be a daunting task
that barely anyone has time to perform.

No worries! Our app is designed to help you make more informed choices about the products you buy,
and the impacts they have on the environment. With our app, you can easily research on your consumption habits,
learn about sustainable alternatives and the level of corporate social responsibility (CSR) of companies,
and connect with local producers to support local businesses. All in one place.

# Why bother?

One may argue that the climate change and social injustice are all fault of evil corporations.
Yes, sure, but there are many small companies doing things right. They fail the competition
with big corporations because more sustainable processes and fair wages make their products
more expensive. But supporting their quality work is the only way
to achieve more open, more transparent and more circular economy for everyone. 🤝

We believe that even if a small percentage of consumers changes their consumption habits
to be more responsible, that will be a market force significant enough that even the laziest companies will
want to change their ways and become more sustainable out of fear of loosing customers.

# What are we going to do?

In the first phase of this project (now) we are aggregating all publicly available information about

 - sustainability of products
 - level of corporate social responsibility of companies

to have all of the information needed to make responsible consumer decisions in one place.

The data we need to collect is very fragmented and incomplete, which makes this task very challenging.

As the project gains momentum and more of your support,
we hope more and more companies will become more open about their processes and help up
create together a distributed database of verified products and services.

We are committed to making all our data open to everyone and we open the source of all the software we develop.
